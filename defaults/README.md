# Default poses

Tuned in game (Camera page, dev_keys=1, numpad) and saved by the plugin. Copy them to
`<Starfield>\Data\SFSE\Plugins\` next to `PhoneTest.dll`:

- `PhoneTest_camera_offset.txt`: the phone's pose while the Camera page is shown (moved out of the cropped area).
- `PhoneTest_arm_pose.txt`: rotation offsets of the left arm bones (clavicle, upper arm, forearm, wrist) for the
  photo pose; they blend in over 0.35 s with the phone pose.
