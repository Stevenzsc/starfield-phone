[size=5][b]StarPhone Pro Max[/b][/size]
[i]A real, in-world phone for your character. Press a key, pull it out, and use it.[/i]

With StarPhone Pro Max, your character takes a phone out of their pocket and holds it in first person. The screen is live and fully usable: check your quests, take photos, play your own music, find your crew and outposts, all without opening a single menu. The UI is styled after the game's own menus, and the phone speaks English or Chinese.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Pull out the phone[/b] with F7 (rebindable) or by holding D-pad down on a controller. The phone sits in your left hand with a glowing screen. A drawn weapon is holstered first.
[*][b]Home screen[/b] with 13 apps, a big clock and the in-game date.
[*][b]Camera[/b]: raise the phone like a real camera. A live viewfinder shows on the screen, the HUD hides, and Enter takes the shot. Take a selfie with a companion, or snap your ship before you leave.
[*][b]Gallery[/b]: browse every photo by date, from the phone camera and from photo mode. Each phone photo is tagged with the place it was taken and its GPS coordinates. Albums by place, full-screen view, and delete from the phone.
[*][b]Music[/b]: play your own music in game. Drop mp3 / wav / wma / m4a / aac / flac files into a folder and they show up as a playlist, with play / pause, previous / next, progress and volume.
[*][b]Quests[/b]: active quests grouped like the mission menu (Main, Faction, Side, Activities), with a details page. Track or untrack a quest from the phone.
[*][b]Messages[/b]: notifications for new objectives and level-ups, with a banner and an unread badge.
[*][b]Contacts[/b]: your crew and following companions. Call one to bring them to you.
[*][b]Scanner[/b]: a radar of nearby characters, with the direction and distance of each. Range 50 m and up.
[*][b]Outposts[/b]: a list of all your outposts.
[*][b]Character[/b]: name, level, XP, credits, health, oxygen and carry limit at a glance.
[*][b]Ship and Star Map[/b]: ship status, grav jump range and your current system.
[*][b]Calendar[/b]: the in-game date and time.
[*][b]Settings[/b]: brightness, open key, controller open button, vibration, language. Saved between sessions.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Install with a mod manager, or extract the archive into your Starfield [b]Data[/b] folder by hand. Launch the game through SFSE.
Install or update only while the game is closed.

[b]Music:[/b] put your music files in [b]Documents\My Games\Starfield\PhoneMusic[/b] (the phone creates the folder the first time you open Music), then reopen the Music app. Playback uses the codecs Windows already has.

[size=4][b]Controls[/b][/size]
[b]Keyboard[/b]
[list]
[*][b]F7[/b]: take out / put away the phone (change it in Settings or in the ini)
[*][b]Arrow keys[/b]: move the selection
[*][b]Enter[/b]: open / confirm / take a photo
[*][b]Backspace[/b]: back
[*]Music: Left / Right change the track, Enter plays or pauses, Up / Down change the volume
[*]Scanner: Left / Right change the range
[/list]
[b]Controller[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Hold D-pad down[/b] (0.5 s): take out the phone (change it in Settings)
[*][b]D-pad[/b]: move, [b]A[/b]: confirm, [b]B[/b]: back. B on the home screen puts the phone away.
[/list]
While the phone is out, fighting, jumping, sneaking and activating are blocked, so your buttons only drive the phone. Button prompts on the screen follow the device you use.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
Edit [b]Data\SFSE\Plugins\PhoneTest.ini[/b] to change the open key, brightness, language ([b]language=[/b] 0 auto, 1 Chinese, 2 English) and the screen layout. Most players never need to touch it; the Settings app covers the common options.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Pure SFSE plugin plus loose assets. No plugin (esp/esm) slot is used, and it does not edit any records.
[*]Safe to add to an existing save. To uninstall, delete the files; nothing is written to your save.
[*]Mods that replace first-person animations may change how the phone sits in your hand.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Known issues[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Some animation states can briefly show the hand out of place.
[*]Photos taken in photo mode outside the phone have no location tag ("Location unknown").
[*]Activity names come from objective text, because the game's own names for them are placeholders.
[/list]
